Meet Gregory and Valerie Stroup. Two distinct practices, with a shared commitment to the people they represent.

Personal injury & wrongful death

Gregory M. Stroup

Gregory Stroup represents individuals and families in personal injury and wrongful death matters. His practice includes vehicle collisions, dangerous property and roadways, animal attacks, defective products and workplace injuries.

Born in Stockton, Gregory began his legal career in 1990 as a law clerk at Stroup & Bakerink, the Tracy firm his father, James Stroup, co-founded. After his admission to the California Bar in 1992, he continued with the practice and established The Stroup Law Firm in 2001.

Gregory has served, pro bono, as a Judge Pro Tem in San Joaquin County and as a board member for the Boys & Girls Club of Tracy. He has held leadership roles with consumer attorney groups, including a term as president of the San Joaquin County Trial Lawyers Association in 2000.

Education & background

  • Juris Doctor, Humphreys College School of Law, 1992
  • BS, Business Administration, CSU Stanislaus, cum laude, 1988
  • Admitted to the California State Bar in 1992

Divorce & family law

Valerie C. Stroup

Valerie Stroup represents individuals and families in divorce and family-law litigation. Her work includes child custody and visitation, child and spousal support, modifications, guardianships and adoption.

Born in Orléans, France, Valerie was admitted to the California Bar in 1992 and practiced as an associate in Northern California before becoming a shareholder in The Stroup Law Firm in 2001. Her background also includes personal injury and commercial litigation.

Valerie has served, pro bono, as a Judge Pro Tem in San Joaquin County and as a board member for the Boys & Girls Club of Tracy. She has been active in Consumer Attorneys of California and the San Joaquin County Bar Association.

Education & background

  • Juris Doctor, Humphreys College School of Law, 1992
  • BS, Mathematics, with a business administration minor, CSU Stanislaus, magna cum laude, 1988
  • Admitted to the California State Bar in 1992
