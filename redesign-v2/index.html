Two generations of Stroup attorneys

Built by a father.
Carried on
by his son.

Jim Stroup worked his way through law school and opened his own practice at 31. In 1987 he partnered with G. Archer Bakerink to form Stroup & Bakerink in Tracy. His son Gregory joined as a law clerk in 1990 and became an associate after passing the bar in 1992.

After Jim died suddenly in 1995, Gregory and Archer Bakerink kept the practice going. In 2001, Gregory founded The Stroup Law Firm, and Valerie Stroup became a shareholder.

Today Gregory handles personal injury and wrongful death cases. Valerie handles divorce and family law.
