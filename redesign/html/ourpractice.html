A Tracy law practice built around individuals, families and the legal challenges that change their lives.

A local practice.
A lasting commitment.

The Stroup Law Firm represents clients in personal injury and family law, including negotiations, mediation, arbitration and trial. The practice serves Tracy and surrounding Northern California communities, including Stockton, Modesto and Sacramento.

Gregory and Valerie Stroup bring distinct areas of focus to one firm. That means a clear connection between your needs and the attorney whose practice is centered on them.

If the firm cannot handle a particular matter, its existing practice is to help direct clients to another firm that can.

1987

James Stroup and G. Archer Bakerink establish Stroup & Bakerink in Tracy.

1992

Gregory and Valerie are admitted to the California State Bar.

2001

The Stroup Law Firm is established, with Gregory and Valerie as shareholders.
