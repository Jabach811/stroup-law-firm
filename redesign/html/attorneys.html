Meet Gregory and Valerie Stroup. Two distinct practices, with a shared commitment to the people they represent.

Personal injury & wrongful death

Gregory M. Stroup

Gregory Stroup represents individuals and families in personal injury and wrongful death matters. His practice includes vehicle collisions, dangerous property and roadways, animal attacks, defective products and workplace injuries.

Born in Stockton, Gregory began his legal career as a law clerk with Stroup & Bakerink in 1990. After his admission to the California Bar in 1992, he continued with the practice and established The Stroup Law Firm in 2001.

His published biography describes service as a Judge Pro Tem in San Joaquin County, involvement with consumer advocacy organizations and community service with the Boys & Girls Club of Tracy.

Education & background

  • Juris Doctor, Humphreys College School of Law, 1992
  • BS, Business Administration, CSU Stanislaus, cum laude, 1988
  • Admitted to the California State Bar in 1992

Divorce & family law

Valerie C. Stroup

Valerie Stroup represents individuals and families in divorce and family-law litigation. Her work includes child custody and visitation, child and spousal support, modifications, guardianships and adoption.

She was admitted to the California Bar in 1992 and became a shareholder in The Stroup Law Firm in 2001. Her background also includes personal injury and commercial litigation.

Her published biography describes service as a Judge Pro Tem in San Joaquin County and as a board member for the Boys & Girls Club of Tracy, along with involvement in professional legal organizations.

Education & background

  • Juris Doctor, Humphreys College School of Law, 1992
  • BS, Mathematics, with a business administration minor, CSU Stanislaus, magna cum laude, 1988
  • Admitted to the California State Bar in 1992
